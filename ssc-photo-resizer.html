Official SSC Photo & Signature Criteria

The Staff Selection Commission (SSC) enforces stringent specifications on candidate image uploads. Files failing these precise bounds are rejected by the automated validation system:

Requirement SSC Photograph Specs SSC Signature Specs
File Size Boundary 20 KB to 50 KB 10 KB to 20 KB
Prescribed Dimensions 3.5 cm (Width) × 4.5 cm (Height) 4.0 cm (Width) × 2.0 cm (Height)

Background & Quality Plain light / white background, front-facing Black ink on crisp white sheet without lines

Top 5 Reasons SSC Applications Get Rejected (And How to Avoid Them)

  • 1. File Exceeds 50 KB or is Under 20 KB: The SSC server will display an immediate validation error and refuse form progression. DocReducer automatically calculates the exact binary curve so your photo lands safely between 30 KB and 48 KB.
  • 2. Wearing Caps, Spectacles, or Masks: SSC instructions strictly state that eyes and ears must be fully visible. Sunglasses or spectacles with reflection/glare lead to immediate disqualification.
  • 3. Blurry or Pixelated Signature: Resizing images with cheap percentage tools often causes severe blurriness. DocReducer preserves stroke luminance using high-order Discrete Cosine Transform (DCT).
  • 4. Horizontal / Upside-Down Signature: Always verify that the signature is upright and clearly legible before hitting upload. You can rotate photos by 90° directly inside DocReducer.
  • 5. Wrong Format (PNG instead of JPG): Even if the file size is under 50KB, if you upload a `.png` file renamed as `.jpg`, the SSC MIME-type inspector will flag it. DocReducer exports true 100% compliant JPEG streams.

Frequently Asked Questions on SSC Photo & Signature

Can I take the photo directly from my mobile phone?

Yes. You can take a selfie or ask someone to capture your portrait against a plain white wall in well-lit daylight, upload it to DocReducer, and size it down to under 50 KB in 1 second.

Does SSC require the candidate name and date printed on the photo?

In recent notifications (such as SSC CGL and CHSL), SSC no longer requires the date of photo (DOP) printed on the photograph unless specifically stated in that notification. However, the photo must not be more than 3 months old from the notification publication date.

Is my photo safe when using DocReducer?

Yes, 100% safe. DocReducer executes compression inside your phone or computer memory (HTML5 Canvas). Your sensitive ID photo is never uploaded to any server.
