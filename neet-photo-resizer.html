Official NTA NEET UG Document Criteria

The National Testing Agency (NTA) strictly regulates all document uploads for the NEET UG medical entrance examination. Files that do not strictly comply with size boundaries, format, or background colors are automatically rejected during application submission:

Document Type Required File Size Prescribed Dimensions Accepted Format Mandatory Specifications
Passport Photograph 10 KB – 200 KB 3.5 cm × 4.5 cm (2.5 × 3.5 in) JPG / JPEG Plain white background, 80% face coverage, ears visible, name & date of photo at bottom
Postcard Size Photo 10 KB – 200 KB 4.0 × 6.0 inches (10 × 15 cm) JPG / JPEG Identical to passport photo with name & DOP. High clarity without borders
Candidate Signature 4 KB – 30 KB 4.0 cm × 2.0 cm (approx 140 × 60 px) JPG / JPEG Black ink on white paper, running handwriting. NO capital letters
Fingers & Thumb Impression 10 KB – 200 KB Standard sheet scan JPG / JPEG All 10 fingers (Left & Right hand) in blue ink on clean white paper without smudging
Class 10 Certificate 50 KB – 300 KB Standard A4 document PDF Document Scanned copy of 10th pass certificate / mark sheet with legible roll number and dates
Category / PwBD Certificate 50 KB – 300 KB Standard A4 document PDF Document Official certificate issued by competent authority in PDF format

Top 6 Reasons NEET Applications Get Rejected (And How to Avoid Them)

  • 1. File Outside the 10 KB – 200 KB Boundary: The NTA portal throws an immediate validation error if your passport or postcard photo is above 200 KB or below 10 KB. DocReducer uses an automated binary search engine that guarantees your compressed file lands safely inside the target range (e.g. 50 KB or 100 KB).
  • 2. Missing Candidate Name and Date of Photograph (DOP): NTA NEET guidelines explicitly require that the candidate's full name and the date of taking the photograph (taken on or after January 1st of the application year) be clearly printed across the bottom portion of the photograph.
  • 3. Non-White Background: Blue, red, or outdoor backgrounds are strictly rejected. The photo must be captured against a plain white background with 80% face coverage from forehead to chin, with both ears clearly visible.
  • 4. Wearing Caps, Masks, or Polarized Spectacles: Caps, hats, tinted goggles, or spectacles with reflection/glare are strictly prohibited. Plain prescription spectacles are permissible only if eyes are completely visible without glare.
  • 5. Signature in Full Capital / Block Letters: NTA guidelines clearly state: “Signatures in all CAPITAL letters are not acceptable and will lead to disqualification.” Only natural running cursive handwriting signed with a black ink ballpoint pen is permitted.
  • 6. Smudged Finger and Thumb Impressions: Left and right hand fingers and thumb impressions must be stamped cleanly with blue stamp ink on white paper, showing clear ridge lines without overlapping.

Frequently Asked Questions on NTA NEET Photo & Signature

Is it mandatory to have candidate name and date printed on the NEET photo?

Yes. NTA NEET UG information bulletins instruct candidates that passport size and 4×6 postcard size photographs should preferably indicate the candidate's name along with the date of taking the photograph. The photograph should not be older than 3 months.

What is the difference between NEET passport photo and postcard size photo?

Both photos must feature the exact same photograph and identical white background. The only difference is the physical dimension: passport photo is standard 3.5 × 4.5 cm (2.5 × 3.5 inches), while the postcard photo is 4 × 6 inches. Both must be uploaded in JPG format between 10 KB and 200 KB.

Can I sign with a blue pen or must it be black ink?

NTA guidelines specifically require the signature to be executed in black ink pen on clean white paper. For fingers and thumb impressions, blue stamp ink on white paper is prescribed.
